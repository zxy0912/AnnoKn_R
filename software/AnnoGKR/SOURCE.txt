The original R/AnnoGK.R, R/GhostKnockoff.R, and R/KF_anno.R are preserved.
R/AnnoGK_dss.R adds four functions from Xiangyu Zhang's historical GK_anno.R:
  GK_anno_dss
  sol_beta_and_lam_ps_dss
  cv_func_dss
  GhostKnockoff.lambda
Only GK_anno_dss is exported; the three helpers are internal.

Reproducibility scope:
This addition supplies the missing DSS implementation. The historical simulation
drivers still require path/dependency configuration and migration of their
source() calls before they can run using only installed packages. Numerical
equivalence of all manuscript results has not been established. Preserve the
historical data, seed sequence and software environment for result comparisons.
